SlamFrames v3.1.0

- Reworked Party Frame event handling to prevent full-frame rebuilds during routine health, aura, and resource traffic.
- Party health changes now update only the affected member's health presentation.
- Party aura changes update the relevant debuff alert without rebuilding the full member frame.
- Added optional Party-only Mana, Rage, Energy, and Focus resource bars.
- Resource events update only the affected member's resource strip and are coalesced/throttled for combat performance.
- Added Party -> Resource Bar ON/OFF setting and /sf partypower on/off.
- Coalesced repeated party roster/leader events into one queued refresh path.
- Staggered initial Party Frame population and circular portrait construction to reduce group-join hitching.
- Removed continuous Blizzard party-frame suppression from the normal reconciliation loop.
- Preserved the 3.0.1 Normal click/menu fixes and the 3.0.2 click-default / Ornate cast-bar hotfixes.

SlamFrames v3.0.2

- Refined the approved Ornate cast-bar artwork for Light and Dark skins.
- Removed two unwanted internal guide/divider lines from the Ornate cast-bar frame.
- Factory and Reset All click-casting defaults now clear all spell/item names, including the Left Click field.
- Preserved working Normal unit-frame left/right clicks, Vanilla-compatible unit menus, spell/item click casting, Party/Raid frames, heal prediction, 1080p/4K artwork, and per-character settings.

SlamFrames v3.0.1

- Fixed right-click context-menu dispatch in the SuperWoW / OctoWoW environment with a standalone Vanilla-compatible fallback.
- Preserved custom click-casting spell and item bindings.
- Fixed Cast Bar settings layout overlap between Preview States and Ornate Flavor Text.
